### SECTION 1: PURPOSE & GENERAL OVERVIEW
Oheneba Media is the sole developer and proprietor of **Skoola**, a comprehensive role-based school management system (the "Software"). The School wishes to implement and utilize Skoola to automate and manage its academic, administrative, financial, and student data systems.

This Agreement sets forth the terms, conditions, payment structures, and service level guidelines under which the Service Provider will deploy Skoola for the School's use.

### SECTION 2: THE 3-MONTH PILOT PROGRAM
1. The Service Provider shall grant the School access to a custom-configured instance of the Skoola platform for a **Pilot Period of three (3) months** starting from the date of setup.
2. During the Pilot Period, the Service Provider will provide standard cloud hosting, configuration, training, and database initialization support.
3. Oheneba Media will configure a custom subdomain (e.g. `[schoolname].skoola.com`) for the School's exclusive use.

### SECTION 3: SUBSCRIPTION FEES & PAYMENT TERMS
The School agrees to the subscription model and payment schedule detailed below:
1. **Annual Subscription Model:** Skoola is provided on a recurring annual subscription basis. The service and license must be renewed every year.
2. **Upfront Payment:** The School is required to pay a minimum of **fifty percent (50%)** of the total annual subscription fee *before* setup on the site and before active usage of the platform is initiated.
3. **Pilot Period Balance:** The remaining **fifty percent (50%)** of the annual subscription fee must be paid in full on or before the final day of the 3-month Pilot Period.
4. **Subscription Renewals:** In subsequent years, the annual subscription renewal fee must be paid in full prior to the start of the renewal term to guarantee uninterrupted service.

### SECTION 4: DEFAULT AND SUSPENSION
1. If the remaining fifty percent (50%) payment is not received by Oheneba Media at the end of the 3-month Pilot Period, the School's access to the Skoola portal may be temporarily suspended.
2. Data will be safely preserved for up to thirty (30) days during suspension, after which Oheneba Media reserves the right to terminate access permanently.

### SECTION 5: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & SECURITY
1. All intellectual property, source code, designs, and proprietary workflows of Skoola remain the exclusive property of Mr. Oheneba Michael Baah and Oheneba Media.
2. The School is granted a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to access the platform. Under no circumstances shall the School attempt to reverse-engineer, copy, or redistribute any part of the Software.
3. Oheneba Media shall employ industry-standard encryption and security protocols to safeguard the School's records and student information.

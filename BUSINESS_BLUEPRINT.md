## Phase 1: Data Profiling & Segmentation (Weeks 1-2)

### 1.1 Outbound Segmentation Matrix

To maximize closing rates, the database of 2,000+ contacts must be divided into three operational segments:

**Segment A: High-End Enterprise Academies (Top 20%)**
- **Profile:** Large school systems (500+ students), private or international funding structures, and active IT administrators.
- **Value Focus:** Custom subdomains (e.g. schoolname.skoola.com), multi-campus reporting, and bespoke service level agreements (SLAs).
- **Outreach Lead:** Directly handled by the Founder (Mr. Oheneba Michael Baah) to convey high trust and prestige.

**Segment B: Mid-Market Scale (Middle 50%)**
- **Profile:** Growing schools (200-500 students) with semi-digital structures looking to completely digitize grades, fee tracking, and parent reports.
- **Value Focus:** 14-day rapid deployment, standard data migration spreadsheets, and dedicated support desk contacts.
- **Outreach Lead:** Inside sales reps utilizing structured scripts and product walkthrough calls.

**Segment C: Inbound/Self-Service Volume (Bottom 30%)**
- **Profile:** Small schools (<200 pupils) or community schools with limited budgets.
- **Value Focus:** Low cost of entry, self-guided setup videos, and self-service Excel data import guides.
- **Outreach Lead:** High-volume automated outbound sequences pointing to educational technology webinars.

### 1.2 Information Enrichment
Prior to calling or emailing, verify the following details for high-priority targets:
- Current student enrollment count.
- Current grading/financial administrative systems in place.
- Names and contact details of the Principal, Registrars, and IT coordinators.
- Procurement/billing cycle milestones (aligned with school terms).

**LinkedIn Executive Connections:**

## Phase 3: Product Demonstration & Closing (Weeks 9-16)

### 3.1 The Custom Mockup Sales Process

Instead of standard, generic software walkthroughs, we implement a **High-Conversion Mockup Demo**:

1. **Pre-Demo Mockup Setup:** Configures a live testing portal loaded with mock data containing the target school's name and classes.
2. **Interactive Walkthrough:** Focuses on the specific pain point identified during the qualification call (e.g. grading accuracy, receipt tracking, or parent communication).
3. **Closing Offer:** Presents standard monthly or termly pricing plans along with free data migration and technical training during the onboarding period.
4. **Contract Sign-off:** Executes a direct Pilot Program Agreement detailing support SLAs and invoice timelines.

### 3.2 Pricing & Direct Subscriptions

All software licenses are directly billed by your enterprise, capturing 100% of the margins:
- **Free Basic Tier:** Limited features for small volume self-setup.
- **Standard Scale Tier:** Full grading and student management capabilities.
- **Premium Enterprise Tier:** Unlimited accounts, custom subdomain setups, and high-priority technical SLAs.

## Phase 4: System Onboarding & Success (Weeks 17-24)

### 4.1 Deployment Timeline

**Weeks 1-2: Data Migration Phase**
- Provide the school with standard bulk upload templates.
- Support staff checks, cleans, and uploads the student and teacher roster into the database.

**Weeks 3-4: Training & Launch**
- Hold video training sessions for teachers and staff.
- Set up system configurations (term structures, grading weights, invoicing profiles).
- Broadcast launch notices and logins to parents.

**Month 2-3: Performance Optimization**
- Track weekly usage metrics and transaction logs.
- Provide quick support resolutions to prevent staff frustration and secure high user adoption.

## Phase 5: Scaling & Network Growth (Months 7-12)

### 5.1 School-to-School Referral Incentives
- Implement a direct school referral incentive: existing clients who introduce peer schools receive a subscription credit (e.g. 1 month free) once the referred school completes onboarding.
- This drives viral organic expansion within regional school networks.

### 5.2 Regional Cluster Marketing
- Host local educational technology roundtables or user webinars.
- Leverage successful deployments in Segment A schools to drive outbound marketing campaigns targeting surrounding institutions in the same district.
